The pricing engine, explained

01

Base & demand

Base price × the number of days you book, then adjusted by how in-demand the item currently is: low demand can drop the price below base, peak demand adds up to 45%.

02

Timing

Book 14+ days ahead and receive a 10% early-bird discount. Book within 48 hours and a 15% last-minute charge applies instead. Weekend days add a per-day premium.

03

Discounts

Stays of 7+ days save 5%, and 14+ days save 12%, calculated on the adjusted subtotal. A valid coupon code is applied last, on top of everything else.
